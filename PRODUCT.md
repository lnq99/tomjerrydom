# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Russian-speaking buyers of vape and e-liquid products, primarily urban, 20-35. Shopping from a phone or desktop at home or on the go. They know what they want; they're not being introduced to vaping — they're choosing where to buy. Trust, price, and speed matter more than education.

## Product Purpose

Tom&Jerry Дом is a Moscow-based vape and e-liquid retail shop (physical + online). The storefront's job is to convert a first-time visitor into a buyer and a buyer into a repeat customer. Success: someone lands, sees the product range, trusts the shop, and places an order.

## Brand Personality

Direct, urban, unpretentious. Not trying to be cool — just reliable and well-stocked. The shop that doesn't waste your time. Voice: confident short sentences, no filler, Russian-first.

## Anti-references

- Dark smoke + neon glow (generic vape shop cliché — overused, dated)
- Purple-to-blue gradients, particle effects, tech-startup visual language
- Luxury fashion editorial: cold all-black minimalism, excessive whitespace as a flex
- Generic big-product-photo-plus-"Shop Now" hero with no personality

## Design Principles

1. **Show the product, earn the trust.** Real imagery and video before anything else. The hero's job is not to introduce the brand — it's to make the viewer want to browse.
2. **Russian-first, always.** Copy in Russian. No decorative English. Dates, numbers, and currency in Russian format.
3. **Direct over clever.** Button labels say exactly what happens. No "Discover more", no "Explore". В каталог. О магазине.
4. **Urban without attitude.** Confident, not intimidating. Street-feel through typography weight and composition, not darkness and neon.
5. **Mobile is the primary surface.** Layout and touch targets built phone-first, desktop is secondary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast. Captions or muted-play for all autoplay video. Reduced-motion: disable Ken Burns and crossfade animations, fall back to instant cut.
